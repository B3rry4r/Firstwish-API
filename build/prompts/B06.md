Cases (input -> expected), beyond P5-COMMON's per-endpoint tests:
 1. Draft: POST makes a draft owned by the caller; PATCH saves parts of it (kind, occasion, names, content document, style, opening,
    ending, when, keep) and is safe to send often (the page autosaves); a stale write (old `version`) is 409 with the current
    version, never a silent overwrite; content is validated as a document (unknown top-level keys refused; size limit from config;
    the passcode never stored in content: Wish.passcodeHash, Q8).
    Files the content names are also written to a join table with foreign keys (ruling Y5; if the schema has none, HALT and
    say so: the lead adds it); a file id that is not the caller's own confirmed file is 422.
 2. Limits come from the ticket's entitlements (photos, chapters, voices): over the limit is 422 naming the limit; an unpaid draft
    may be edited freely within Starter limits.
 3. Seal: only a paid wish (its ticket fulfilled: until B07 merges, a test sets that state through the service) with what its kind
    requires; sealing issues the wish number (gapless, no two wishes share one even when 20 seal at once: test it) and the link slug
    (unguessable, 8-32 characters), writes a WishTransition, and fixes the open time in UTC from the sender's chosen local time and zone.
 4. The state machine is one table of allowed moves (draft, sealed, live, resting, erased, taken down): any other move is refused and
    every move writes a WishTransition with who or what caused it. Time moves it: at the open time sealed becomes live; at term end
    live becomes resting; after the hold (365 days) resting is erased (personal content removed, the row and its number kept); a
    restore within its window brings resting back to live. All by the clock port in tests.
 5. Jobs frame: a due-jobs table polled by a BullMQ worker, each job safe to run twice (a job that ran is not run again; two workers
    never run one job: test with two), failures retried with backoff up to the declared tries then parked for staff (Job, JobRun).
    Export `jobs.schedule(kind, at, key)` for other bundles.
 4b. Any move that takes a wish out of sight (taken down, removed, erased) also clears its saves in the same transaction
    (SavedWishesService.forget, ruling BB6) and makes its link answer gone.
 6. List and detail are the owner's (query-level check); a business login sees its business's wishes only as the registry says;
    GET /v1/wishes/:id never serves a receiver (that is B10's /v1/w/:slug).
