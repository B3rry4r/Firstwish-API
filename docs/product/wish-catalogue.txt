Rules for anything a customer reads:
- Say kind. Never format, category or type.
- Celebration is the kind's name. "For family and friends" is its line.
- Never say abroad or diaspora. The feature is stated once: every wish opens at midnight in their time zone.
- Never say launch, coming or a release date. The only exception is the Soon tag on a greyed entry.
- Firstwish is not a product for one country. Outside the style names, copy avoids words only one country would know.
- Never say forever about how long a wish is kept. A wish has a term. When it ends the wish rests, and it can be kept.
## How to read this
Firstwish ships six kinds of wish. A kind is a fixed arrangement of chapters, a finale, a default style and a sender. Within a kind, each occasion changes the words, the timing and sometimes one chapter, never the engine. This is the catalogue of all of it: what each occasion is, who sends it, what plays, which style it opens in, and what it sells for.
Two rules hold everywhere:
- The sender picks the kind, then the occasion. Firstwish arranges the chapters. The sender can switch any chapter on or off in the app and change the style with one tap.
- Every kind ends with the same two things: the moment the receiver reacts (the candle, the ring box, the scratch card), and the Firstwish mark. That is how a wish spreads.

## Tickets, terms and extras
No ticket is measured in years. Moment lives 7 days, Keepsake 2 weeks, Signature 1 month and Premiere 2 months. The clock starts when the wish is first opened, not when it is bought or sealed. Wedding wishes count their term from the wedding date, so a Save the date lives until the day and then for its ticket's term.
Moment cannot be kept; its one option is to become a Keepsake for the difference. Keepsake, Signature and Premiere can be kept, by the sender or by the receiver. A keep is one more month, six months or a year.
A ticket comes with one style, the sender's pick, from the ticket's level or a level below: Starter on Moment, Standard on Keepsake, Premium on Signature and Premiere. No ticket gives every style. Video clips come only with Premiere, in premium styles made for video. A wish holds 20 of the sender's photos at most.
What each ticket adds: Keepsake, a voice note or song link, a notice when the wish is opened, and a reaction from the receiver. Signature, the portrait video for WhatsApp status, a print-ready QR card, and up to ten friends who each add a message, a voice note and one photo. Premiere, three video clips inside the wish.
Firstwish Concierge is a service, not a ticket. Our team makes the wishes. It is built for many at once, a team, a guest list or a client list, and priced by how many are sent. One wish made for a customer is a Signature wish, delivered within three hours: ₦25,000 at list, ₦15,000 at the launch price.
What happens when a term ends:
- Three days before the term ends, the sender is told and offered a keep.
- On the last day the wish rests. Anyone opening the link sees the seal and "This wish is resting. Wake it." A Pro wish's resting screen still shows "Photos by" or "Arranged by" and the booking button.
- The wish stays in the sender's account under Resting. Either person can pay to wake it.
- A resting wish is held for 12 months, then erased. Before that, both are offered the video file.
- On the wish's anniversary the sender gets a message to wake it.
The Pass keeps every wish its holder has made live for as long as the Pass is active. A published wish stays live while it is public, and the wishes on a business's page stay live while its Pro plan is active.
Delete is soft. A deleted wish leaves the account and the link at once, can be restored for 30 days, and is then erased. Erase now removes it for good within 24 hours.
Extras are listed once on the pricing page and offered in the app at the moment they matter. An extra is never a cheaper way to reach the next ticket:
- Moment has no extras, and it cannot be kept. Its one option is to become a Keepsake for the difference.
- Keepsake has a ceiling of ₦7,500 in extras. Past that the app offers Signature for the difference, and extras already paid count toward it. A keep does not count toward the ceiling.
- Signature has no ceiling, and no video. To add clips it becomes a Premiere for the difference.
- Video extras exist on Premiere only.
- A keep is paid by the sender or the receiver. It wakes a resting wish, or extends a live one.

## Now Showing
Every wish is private by default. A creator can publish a wish to Now Showing, the public gallery.
- The sender is the publisher. Only a creator profile with a confirmed phone can publish, and at that moment the sender confirms they have the consent of everyone shown.
- The receiver is told and can stop it. Firstwish messages the receiver directly, and they can refuse or take it down later.
- Anyone shown can take it down. Every public wish has a "This is me, take it down" button. It comes down at once and is reviewed after.
- A public cut. The sender chooses which chapters show. The letter can stay private.
- After it opens, never before. No public wishes about children. Reactions only, no comments.
- Music. A public wish plays music from a licensed library, never the sender's own song.
| Level of sharing | Who can open it | Where it appears |
| Private (default) | Anyone with the link | Nowhere |
| Public link | Anyone, and it can be shared openly | The creator's own page |
| Now Showing | Everyone | The gallery, by kind, occasion and style, and on its style's page |
Every public wish shows its number, its style and the style's creator, with one button: Make one like this.
Showcase wishes are public wishes Firstwish makes with our model's photos, so that every style can be seen as a real wish. Each style's page shows its showcase wishes first, then public wishes made in that style. Wish № 0001 is the first.
An open Group wish is a Group wish with a public invitation in place of ten private ones. It is still sent to one person, and the organiser approves each voice.
## Style Studio
Style Studio is where creators build styles: designers, motion designers and developers. It is a motion tool with a timeline, keyframes, layers, masks, animated type, triggers, the creator's own artwork and sound, and AI that builds and changes scenes from a description. The limits protect the wish: every chapter present, readable type, smooth on a budget phone.
From idea to catalogue: build, preview on sample wishes, submit with the kinds it is made for, automatic checks, review by Firstwish, placement, live.
| Level | Who places it | What the creator gets |
| Starter | Firstwish only. Starter styles are ours |  |
| Standard | Firstwish, on acceptance | Their byline on the style's page. No payment. It can be promoted |
| Premium | Firstwish, on evidence | 20% of the premium style price each time a paid wish uses it, paid monthly past a threshold |
Creators do not set prices. Every quarter, styles move between Standard and Premium on how often a preview in that style becomes a paid wish.
A premium style is ₦4,000 at list price, so each paid wish in a creator's Premium style pays them ₦800, or ₦480 while the 40% off runs. A premium style can be made for video, built to hold clips; Premiere wishes use those. The creator's share on Pass and Pro wishes is not set yet.
Templates: Firstwish's own starter templates are free to every creator. Opening a Premium style as a template needs a paid creator plan. The original creator decides whether their style can be a template, and gets a quarter of the royalty when a style built from it earns.
| What creators pay | Starting price | What it buys |
| Free | ₦0 | One draft, a small credit allowance, no submission |
| Creator plan | ₦7,500 a month | Unlimited drafts, a monthly credit allowance, two reviews a month, premium templates |
| Credit pack | ₦3,000 | More AI actions |
| Extra review | ₦2,500 | One more submission in a month |
| Signed style | ₦5,000 a month | Their byline on the last screen of every wish made in their styles |

## Group
Many people to one person. The organiser starts the wish; up to 10 friends add a message, a voice note up to 60 seconds, and one photo each, through one private link. Nobody needs an account. Friends never see the wish or each other's pieces. Chapters: Seal, Title, Voices wall, a note from the organiser, Finale. Default style Pidgin Gist; Owambe for a parent's big birthday done by the children.
How it runs: the organiser sets a deadline (default 6 hours before it opens) and one question ("Your favourite memory with her"). Firstwish makes the link and a ready WhatsApp message. The organiser sees pieces arrive, hides or reorders them, nudges whoever is late. At midnight the pieces play as one film.

## Shoot Reveal
A photographer, or another creative, to a client. It replaces the Google Drive link. Chapters: Cover, Film, Favourites, From the day, Get your photos, Credits, Book again, and the Firstwish close ("This was a wish. Make one."). The sample built for Fima shot is the reference: an opening that plays once, then the viewer scrolls through the film at their own pace.
The photographer fills ten fields and writes one note: frames (6 to 15), client name, shoot type, city, date, one note, credits (name and handle per role), the full-set link, a booking link, a style. Firstwish reads lens, shutter and aperture from the files. Ninety seconds from upload to a link.
Who uses it: photographers first. The same format serves anyone whose finished work is a set of images: makeup artists and hairstylists (the bridal look), designers and tailors (the finished outfit), videographers (stills from the film), decorators. The credits chapter already names them on every reveal, so each one sees the product with their own name on it.
